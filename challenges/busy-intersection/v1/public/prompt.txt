Build an original, polished browser experience for a living four-way signalized
Busy Intersection. The submission is a static offline artifact whose entry
point is index.html. Do not use a backend or a network dependency; copy every
runtime dependency you use into the submission.

The scene must have four connected approaches and exits, straight/left/right
movements, visible signal phases, four pedestrian crossings with compatible
walk phases, continuous lane-following and turning, bounded vehicle spacing,
braking and queues, and evaluator-supplied vehicle and pedestrian demand.
Include pause, reset, simulation-speed controls and a useful default view.

Expose this evaluator bridge from the browser:

window.__RALPH_BENCH__ = {
  apiVersion: "traffic/v1",
  describeNetwork(),
  loadScenario(scenario),
  reset(seed),
  advance(simulatedMilliseconds),
  snapshot(),
  drainEvents()
};

The evaluator owns demand, seeds, timing, safety checks and authoritative
metrics. Every requested trip must remain accounted for as requested, admitted,
active, completed, or explicitly rejected. Do not silently discard, replace,
shorten, teleport, or move trips. Snapshots and events must describe the same
simulation state used by visible playback; summary counters alone are not
authoritative.

The evaluator checks the connected movement graph, compatible lanes and paths,
vehicle spacing and collisions, red-light entry, vehicle/pedestrian conflicts,
teleportation or invalid disappearance, trip reconciliation, bounded service,
queue storage, completion and recovery after demand is removed. It may inspect
your artifact at standardized desktop dimensions and record an animated
overview. Keep lane geometry, signal state, queue boundaries and critical
distinctions legible.

Completion evidence is physical, not a summary counter: for every completed
trip, emit ordered trip.admitted, vehicle.entered, vehicle.exited and
trip.completed events, and expose at least two snapshots with a changed
vehicle position on its declared movement. A trip status of completed without
this lifecycle and trajectory evidence is invalid.

You may choose a confident 2D, 2.5D or 3D direction. Make the composition,
typography, palette, motion, controls and information hierarchy intentional.
Visual polish is reviewed separately from traffic validity and throughput; no
palette, framework, camera rig, class name or reference architecture is
prescribed.
